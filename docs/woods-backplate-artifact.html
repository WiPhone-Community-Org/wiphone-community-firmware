Before the TPS63020 goes on the plate

The module ships with 3V3 / 4V2 / 5V solder-selectable — set the 3V3 jumper first. Its PS pad is the power-save select: tie it LOW for pulse-skipping at idle (this plate idles around 45 mA) and never leave it floating.

Its EN thresholds are tighter than the TLV62569 it replaces — VIL 0.4 V. The measured 0.22 V divider still passes, but with less headroom than v1 had, so the phone-off EN check below is load-bearing now. Six in the pack: bench one before it goes on the plate.

v2 removes no charging path

The PowerBoost stays. It still charges the phone through W4, still charges its own pack over its own microUSB, and phone-USB still reaches the plate through D1. The only change is that the regulator gained a second source to fall back on.

D2's reverse blocking is safety-critical — it is what stops the pack's 4.9 V from back-feeding VBAT and uncontrolled-charging the phone's cell. Meter the band before power.

Charging paths—all four unchanged

v1 rejected VBAT because the TLV62569 is a buck — it needs > 3.4 V in and a 1S cell ends at 3.0 V, so it would drop out at the bottom of the discharge. That objection was about the part, not the idea: a buck-boost has no such floor.
